About Morabaraba

Morabaraba is a two-player strategy game from Southern Africa, played on three nested squares with diagonals. Each side places twelve cows, then moves them; three in a line make a mill, and every new mill shoots an enemy cow.

Strategy

Build a mill you can open and close: step one cow out and back in, and you shoot again every other turn.

Ways to play

Against the computer, at three levels, or with two players on one device.
